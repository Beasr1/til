## api-orchestration

- [ ] **sagas and reserve/commit** — In reserve/commit flows, pin price-affecting inputs to the version captured at reservation, make compensations total by clamping, route every failure exit through compensation, give reservations a timeout on the owning side, and move dependent steps to terminal states atomically. Target: api-orchestration (04-retries-idempotency-and-durability.md). Verify: Garcia-Molina and Salem 'Sagas'; Helland 'Life beyond Distributed Transactions' and 'Immutability Changes Everything'

- [ ] **outbound clients and payload design** — Put cross-cutting outbound headers in the one client per downstream; treat negative results as re-checkable and serve stale-while-revalidate on forced refresh; keep large binaries out of metadata responses.
